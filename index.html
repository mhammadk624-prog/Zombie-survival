⚔️ SURVIVAL

Endless waves. Random mutators from wave 3. Boss every 5th wave.

⏱ TIME ATTACK

75 seconds, no wave breaks. Every kill adds time. Chase the score.

👹 BOSS RUSH

Bosses only, with all three attack phases. Wave N spawns N bosses.

🎯 SNIPER ONLY

Locked to the sniper. Pierces the whole horde if you line them up.

🔌 HORDE DEFENSE

Zombies ignore you and swarm the generator in the middle of the map. It dies, you lose — so hold the line.

👑 CAMPAIGN

45 waves across 3 acts. The Harbinger rises at wave 20, then The Overlord — 1000 HP, volleys, slams and summons — at wave 45. Beat it to see the credits.

💀 NIGHTMARE

Unlocked by beating Campaign. Endless waves, a mutator active every single wave, harder from the first zombie.
