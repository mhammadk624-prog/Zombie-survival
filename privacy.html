Privacy Policy Zombie Survival

Qazi Games · Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app and web version of Zombie Survival, published by Qazi Games ("we", "us"). The game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not run servers that collect your data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or sell your personal information. The game keeps its data on your own device only, using your browser's local storage: your progress (coins, weapons, skills, best score), settings such as sound, and your daily-reward streak. This data is not sent to us. It stays on your device until you delete it.

2. Permissions and device features

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location. It uses your device's audio output for sound effects and may check the date on your device to give the daily reward.

3. Hosting

The game loads from GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs, under GitHub's own privacy policy. We do not receive this data.

4. Ads, purchases and tracking

The game contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user accounts and no analytics or tracking tools. No data is shared with third parties.

5. Children

Zombie Survival is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The game contains cartoon-style fantasy violence.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Reset or delete saved progress by clearing the app data (see below).
  • To remove all saved data, clear the app's storage in Android: Settings → Apps → Zombie Survival → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

7. Security

Zombie Survival is loaded over HTTPS. Because your saved data stays on your device, its protection also depends on your device's security.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
